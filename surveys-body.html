How the weekly survey works

  1. The Board asksA Board member posts a question about a project and sets an end date, or leaves it open-ended.
  2. You vote once a weekPick a score from 1 to 5. It is locked for the rest of the week, Monday to Sunday.
  3. Monday rolloverThe week's votes are combined into one weekly record with its start date. Individual votes are then deleted.
  4. Everyone sees the trend"Total average" shows last week's result. Open History for every past week.
